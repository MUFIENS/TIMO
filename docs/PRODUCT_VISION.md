# Product Vision — Timo

## Vision

Help people build a clearer, kinder relationship with the time they spend on their computers.

## Mission

Turn ordinary desktop activity into understandable, private, and actionable insight without making the computer feel monitored or judged.

## Positioning

Timo is a friendly desktop Digital Wellbeing companion for people who want to understand their computer habits. It is more approachable than a technical process monitor and more context-aware than a simple stopwatch.

## Official brand tagline

**A little clarity for your digital day.** (**APPROVED — OWNER DECISION**)

Timo should feel gentle, calm, friendly, and clear—making screen time visible without judgment and leaving decisions in the user's hands.

## Product principles

1. **Awareness, not judgment.** Use neutral language and avoid guilt-inducing scores.
2. **Foreground truth.** Count what the user actively has in the foreground, not every process running in the background.
3. **Transparent automation.** Explain what is detected, how it is categorized, and how users can correct it.
4. **Privacy by default.** Local storage and no content surveillance.
5. **Lightweight by design.** Native event-driven tracking; UI rendering is not required for tracking.
6. **Accessible friendliness.** Playful visuals never come at the expense of clarity, contrast, and usability.
7. **Honest classification.** Unknown is better than a confident-looking wrong label.
8. **User control.** Users can edit categories, preferences, data, and tracking behavior.

## Emotional experience

Timo should feel calm, optimistic, gently playful, and respectful. It can celebrate a user-defined goal, but it must not scold users for gaming, entertainment, or long sessions.

## Differentiators

- Foreground-aware time tracking with idle, lock, sleep, and resume behavior.
- Application recognition across installed apps, Store apps, launchers, and standalone executables.
- Per-application detail pages and chronological usage timeline.
- Local-first data handling.
- A distinct animated 3D mascot and Soft Playful Neo-Brutalist visual identity.

## Non-goals

Timo is not employee monitoring, parental surveillance, a keystroke logger, browser history recorder, medical device, productivity judge, antivirus product, or process-killer utility.

## Brand voice

Warm, concise, plain-language, and non-judgmental. Prefer “You spent 2h 15m in Chrome” over “You wasted 2h 15m.” Prefer “Take a break when it feels right” over commands or warnings.
